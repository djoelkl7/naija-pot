Our Story

Cooked the way
Mama did.

BUKKA is a love song to Nigerian food — where every grain of jollof, every pound of yam, and every smoky stick of suya carries the unmistakable spirit of home.

From our kitchen in Caldecotte to our beloved Suya Restaurant, we serve dishes the way they were meant to be eaten: fresh, hot, deeply seasoned, and made by people who understand that food is more than fuel — it's memory, family, culture on a plate.

Whether you're craving a quiet plate of ewa agoyin or feeding a crowd with party-style jollof, BUKKA welcomes you to the table.
